Two lines, 238 characters: a warning sent back to one specific night — New Year's Day, 2 in the morning, the kitchen, a brother whispering "I need help" — and one sentence tracing what that night cost: he never asked again, he moved out by spring, and five years went into learning how to say sorry to an empty room.

> When your brother whispers "I need help" in the kitchen at 2 in the morning on New Year's Day, do not tell him to sleep on it.
>
> He never asked again, moved out by spring, and you spent five years learning how to say sorry to an empty room.

The warning works because the moment can be pictured — a time, a place, a sentence said out loud — and the second line does not repeat the warning, it follows the consequence: silence, departure, and five years of apology to an empty room.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). Deterministic: the same two lines on every run. No network, no randomness.
